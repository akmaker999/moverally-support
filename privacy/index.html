privacy by design.

This policy explains how MoveRally collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the mobile application.

1. Who is responsible

MoveRally is operated by Anjul in Australia. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to akappdev0@gmail.com.

2. Information MoveRally collects

MoveRally collects only information needed to provide accounts, activity tracking, private groups, leaderboards, challenges and notifications:

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and display name.
  • Profile preferences: time zone, preferred units, activity goals, sharing choices and notification choices.
  • Fitness information you authorise: steps, workout type, workout start and end times, duration, active calories, source application and daily active-day qualification.
  • Social fitness information: private-group membership, group names, invitation state, challenge titles and descriptions, participation, scores, ranks and achievements.
  • Technical information: activity sync status and, when notifications are enabled, the device push-notification token and platform.

3. Apple Health

MoveRally requests read-only access to the Apple Health categories you choose, currently steps, workouts and active energy. MoveRally does not write information to Apple Health.

Fitness information is used to show your activity, build your charts, calculate leaderboards and score challenges. It is not used for advertising, sold to data brokers or used to make medical decisions.

4. How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and keep it available across devices.
  • Display your activity, workouts, progress and achievements.
  • Operate private groups, invitation codes, leaderboards and challenges.
  • Apply your sharing and notification preferences.
  • Protect the service, diagnose sync problems and prevent abuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. What friends can see

Members of the same private group may see your display name, challenge participation and the activity totals you have chosen to share. You can independently turn sharing off for steps, exercise calories and active days.

Friends do not receive your email address, source record identifiers, complete Health records or exact workout times through group leaderboards. When a sharing category is off, other members see that score as unavailable.

6. Service providers

MoveRally uses service providers only to operate the application:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage and server functions.
  • Apple provides Apple Health, TestFlight, App Store distribution and Apple Push Notification service.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this support and privacy website. The website uses no MoveRally cookies or analytics scripts.

These providers process information under their own terms and may process it in countries other than yours.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications, MoveRally stores a device push token linked to your account so invitations and rally updates can be delivered. You can change notification categories in MoveRally and revoke device permission in iPhone Settings.

8. Retention and deletion

Account and activity information is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account & activity data. If you own a private group, you must transfer or delete that group first.

Account deletion removes your active MoveRally profile, stored activity, memberships, notification token and related personal records. Limited information may remain temporarily in encrypted service backups until those backups expire under the provider’s normal retention cycle, or where retention is legally required.

9. Security

MoveRally uses encrypted network connections, authenticated access and database row-level security. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and keep invitation codes within the intended group.

10. Your choices and rights

You can manage Health permissions, activity sharing, notification preferences and account deletion from the app or device settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

11. Children

MoveRally is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information should contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as MoveRally changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email akappdev0@gmail.com.
