MoveRally community standards

Compete kindly.
Keep every rally safe.

These rules apply to display names, private-group names, challenge titles, descriptions and other content shared through MoveRally.

Be respectful

Use respectful display names, private-group names, challenge titles and descriptions. Do not use these shared fields for harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, bullying, impersonation, spam, encouragement of harm, or another person’s private or health information.

Filtering and reports

MoveRally checks display names, group names and challenge text before they are shared. Some abusive or explicit terms may be rejected. Because automatic filtering cannot identify every harmful use, group members can also report display names, group names and challenge content for review.

Report content

Use the safety menu beside a group member, or the report action on a group or challenge. Reports are private and include a server-side snapshot of the reported content. Add only information needed to explain the concern; never include a password or detailed health information.

Block a member

Blocking hides both members from each other’s member lists, scores, leaderboards, standings and new challenge invitations. Manage blocked members under Profile → Privacy & sharing. Group ownership and already-finalised results may require support assistance.

Moderation response

MoveRally reviews reports promptly, with a target of reviewing urgent and safety-related reports within 24 hours. Depending on context and severity, action can include removing content, removing a member from a group, issuing a warning or removing an account. Repeated or serious violations may lead to immediate removal.

Contact

For an urgent safety concern or a question about a moderation decision, email akappdev0@gmail.com. Include the report context but do not send passwords, invitation codes or detailed health information.
